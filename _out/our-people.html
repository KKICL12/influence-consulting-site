Our facilitators

Talented, respected facilitators.

All our people are talented and respected facilitators who have enjoyed successful careers in a variety of sectors. They service clients from New Zealand and beyond.

Kerry Kirton

Auckland, New Zealand

Kerry established Influence Consulting and runs the company as managing director. He has developed relationships with major clients across the Asia Pacific region for many years, and is considered a trusted and strategic advisor who makes meaningful contributions to their organisational goals. His experience, creativity and business acumen mean he is able to tailor solutions that best suit his clients' needs. Kerry holds degrees in law and commerce and practiced as a corporate lawyer for many years prior to establishing Influence Consulting.

James Burkitt

Wellington, New Zealand

James is a director of Influence Consulting. He regularly leads workshops and coaches individuals, both in New Zealand and internationally, in the area of face-to-face communication skills. He works with professionals with frontline business development responsibilities from a wide range of professional service firms. Prior to joining Influence Consulting in 2004, James worked as a lawyer for 10 years, including stints in London, Singapore and Thailand. In his last legal role, he established and built the corporate practice for an international firm in Thailand. This real-world experience ensures that his coaching is relevant, credible and practical. James holds a law degree.

Duncan Ashford

Queenstown, New Zealand

Duncan is passionate about improving business performance and personal success through behavioural change. He provides coaching and training on all aspects of influencing and communication skills, including presentation and relationship selling skills. With over 25 years' experience in senior Sales, Relationship Management and Learning & Development roles with global financial institutions, he understands regional and cultural nuances, having worked in the UK, USA, Hong Kong, China, Singapore, Thailand, Australia and New Zealand. Duncan holds a Bachelor of Arts degree from London University.

Kate Peterson

Christchurch, New Zealand

Kate has worked across a variety of industries, developing skills in sales, operations, customer service, business management, HR and training — including roles with the Progressive Group, Lion Nathan, PGG Wrightson and Meridian Energy as General Manager of Culture and Change. The majority of her work is with leaders and their talented people — those who choose to ensure their investment in each other is maximised, without compromising what really matters to each of them.

John Gelagin

Sydney, Australia

John is a highly professional and experienced facilitator committed to delivering relevant and engaging training. Over the past 8 years he has worked with many hundreds of people, helping them to influence others, deliver engaging presentations and build business development skills. Before joining Influence, John practised law for 8 years with firms in Australia and the UK and spent 5 years with an international publishing company in management roles. John holds a science and law degree.

David Lennane

Melbourne, Australia

David has coached in the area of communication and influencing skills for over 20 years. He assists clients across Australia and New Zealand from his Melbourne base, helping groups and individuals prepare, rehearse and deliver content in critical influencing situations. He specialises in coaching for in-house teams within large organisations, client conversations, and high-stakes bid and tender presentations. His background includes senior marketing and management roles at leading professional service firms and, before that, seven years as a PR consultant in New Zealand and the UK.

Abigail Vink

Wellington, New Zealand

Abigail is an experienced coach and facilitator with a particular focus on the public sector. She supports others as they reflect on their thinking and behavioural patterns and make positive changes to support their professional effectiveness. Abigail holds coaching credentials with the International Coaching Federation, the Institute of Executive Coaching and Leadership, and Strengths Network Aotearoa. She began her career in foreign policy and diplomacy and holds Bachelor of Laws and Bachelor of Arts (Chinese) degrees.

Susan Miller

Auckland, New Zealand

Susan is an experienced coach, facilitator and mediator having predominantly worked in the public health, community and legal sectors. She has spent the past 9 years dedicated to facilitating communications courses that have equipped people to influence their clients and colleagues for better work and life outcomes. Susan has a law degree, is a trained mediator and a licensed RTT practitioner.

Sarah Morton

Auckland, New Zealand

Sarah holds a Bachelor of Arts (Political Science), a Post Graduate Diploma in Drama (Auckland University) and LTCL Effective Speaking (Trinity College). An engaging facilitator, she has worked as a teacher and trainer for 30 years, the last 12 in business-based training, on leadership and business communication programmes for organisations such as Downer, Fisher & Paykel Healthcare, ADHB, Sistema and ETEL. In 2019 she received a “Highly Commended Champion Trainer” Award from Skills Highway.
