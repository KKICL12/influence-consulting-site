Private Sector · Banking

“Kerry’s session was absolutely fantastic! It was highly interactive, engaging, and incredibly interesting.”

Our client in the banking sector needed to develop their leaders' communication and influencing skills. With major changes happening in their sector, including shifts in technology use, it was crucial that leaders were able to effectively communicate and motivate their teams to embrace those changes.

To address this need, we tailored our three core programmes — Be Clear, Engage & Persuade, and Inspire — to the specific needs of our client. Participants attended the programmes based on their individual capabilities and needs, with some choosing to attend each programme gradually over time. Throughout the training, we ensured all participants focused on their specific influencing situations.

Our client was able to point to key changes in the way their leaders communicated, resulting in buy-in to critical changes across the entire organisation.

Private Sector · Infrastructure

“Thank you James. The team definitely found the course very helpful and interesting. They have expressed what a great coach you were for these few days.”

Our client is an owner/operator of significant infrastructure assets, as well as a number of additional businesses.

Participants on our Influencing & Stakeholder Engagement Skills programme are drawn from across the organisation — plant site engineering and operations, client contracting and sales, procurement and finance roles from head office, and everyone in between. Participants bring their own live, current situations into the programme, from discussions with suppliers, customers and regulators to internal propositions around capital allocation, compliance maturity and efficiency initiatives. Their situations are used as a platform to learn our proven concepts and techniques, and for application back in the workplace.

We have delivered the programme across a range of locations for this client since 2014, and it is their top-ranking learning and development course based on staff feedback.

Public Sector · Policy Implementation

“This workshop was fantastic! The content was really helpful for bringing consciousness to the way you interact with and influence people.”

A public sector client faced a critical challenge in implementing Government policy that required major behavioural change. They needed to develop their staff's influencing skills to effectively engage government agencies and the public — a critical requirement, since implementing government policy often requires coordination and cooperation across various agencies and stakeholders.

Our core influencing skills programme was customised to meet the specific needs of the client, enabling participants to work on their own scenarios that required them to influence others to buy in to the proposed change. The programme provided skills including how to establish rapport with stakeholders, the use of persuasive communication techniques, and how to build consensus around the proposed change.

Participants applied the skills they learned in their work to influence others for real buy-in to change, enabling the client to implement Government policy that required major behavioural change.

Public Sector · Government Ministry

“I would definitely recommend this course to others — it's the most relevant and well-delivered piece of training I have undertaken for some time.” — Senior Manager

Our client is a significant Government Ministry with extensive policy, stakeholder engagement and operational responsibilities.

We work with their external and overseas-facing staff, as well as senior professionals from across the organisation who need to influence others around strategic, policy, compliance, technical and safety-related scenarios. Staff participate in our flagship Influencing Skills programme, using our strategies and 7Cs skill sets for stakeholder engagement in situations from informal 1-1 meetings through to multi-party formal discussions.

A recurring theme is the need to influence without the authority of legislation, policy or rules. Through strong working relationships, building credibility on topic, and presenting well-reasoned propositions while remaining alert to the specific needs of their stakeholders, participants gain much more traction. The programme has been run by the Ministry since 2015, with a long waiting list.

Professional Services · Business Development

“No wonder we are hitting our targets! Thanks to James for his transforming work with us.” — Finance Director

Our client is an overseas consultancy firm with global reach — a large number of consultants who are technically excellent and deliver outstanding projects, but are also required to develop new business.

This presents challenges: reticence around feeling 'salesy', difficulties articulating the value the firm can bring, and taking a long-term, consistent approach to developing business while managing the demands of client work.

Through a firm-wide programme of workshops and ongoing coaching, in person and via video, we introduced a clear and reliable methodology for developing new business based on precise client understanding, clear articulation of value, and building strong working chemistry with clients, contacts and champions. The consultants are now much more comfortable and confident with business development, with a higher rate of success in winning new business.

Professional Services · Presentation Skills
